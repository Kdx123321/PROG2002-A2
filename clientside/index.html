Welcome to Liuzhou Care

"No one in Liuzhou should face hardship alone."

Liuzhou Care is a local charitable organisation dedicated to supporting vulnerable families and young people across Liuzhou. Together with the Bright Futures Youth Foundation, we host charity events throughout the year - fun runs, gala dinners, concerts, auctions and more - to raise funds and awareness for the causes that matter most. Our Mission

We provide food relief, housing support, and community programs for families doing it tough, and we partner with the Bright Futures Youth Foundation to mentor and support young people aged 12-25.

Why We Host Events

Every ticket purchased at our events becomes a donation. From a homemade cake at a bake sale to a gala dinner table, your participation directly funds our programs across the city.
